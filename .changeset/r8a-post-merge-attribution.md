R8a: post-merge attribution and the baseline. A merged run now enters `observe` in the commit that spends its integrate grant, and leaves it once the policy's observation window has passed since GitHub's merge time, recorded in P14's record as `observation-closed`. Two templates feed it, and neither seats a model or starts a run. `observe-signal`, valid only on `post-merge` and `ci-failure`, records a push or a failed check against the change whose commit the event names, from the extracted fields alone, and records no candidate. `observe-history`, valid only on `scheduled`, walks the default branch's first-parent history: a later commit whose `git patch-id --stable` is a change's inverse is its rollback, and one that inverts some of its hunks is a partial reversal, reported apart and never counted as a rollback. Every first-parent change no run made is the baseline. Each signal is a write-once `ObservationRecord`, under its run or beside the runs, and each time says whether it is provider-reported, committer-claimed, or the runtime's. R2's report replaces the escaped false-done rate's `'not-established'` with a measured rate over runs whose window closed, and adds the change-failure rate per role and seat beside its baseline, rollback, partial-reversal, and check-failure counts, time to restore kept apart by time source, the baseline's lead time, and a statement that each bound assumes independent runs. `TriggerPolicy` gains `observe.windowMs`, required while an observe template is named; `TemplateId` gains both templates; `TriggerDecision` gains the `observed` outcome; `NightlyRecord` gains `observation`; the Vault gains `recordObservation` and `readObservations`; and the station machine gains the `observe` step.
